## Manual bridges and refusals

The manually reviewed bridges are the conservative base/builtin registry, checked-source projections, event payload
encodings, renderer/export projections, foreign asset adapters, and UI/LSP facts. Their executable rows compare exact
source bytes, normal forms, semantic event tracks, or consumer observations at the strongest relation each owner
promises. Hashes only locate candidates; complete values decide equality.

The refusal/citation checks remain `scripts/check-docs.sh`: Open Music Theory claims resolve by source filename, and the
core/quotation refusals cite their functional-language sources or carry the musical counterexample that prices them.
Passing this audit does not establish a universal music theory, behavioral machine equivalence, foreign-format meaning
beyond the stated support matrices, or a portable real-time deadline.

There are no conformance exceptions. Graduation changes document force only; it adds no surface form, theory, backend,
or runtime path.
