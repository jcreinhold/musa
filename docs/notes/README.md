# The notes

**Status: governs nothing.** Nothing here decides what musa is or what gets built. It is kept because an argument is
worth more next to its refutation than deleted, and because a toolchain trap that cost someone a day should cost the
next person a minute.

| Directory | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [`research/`](research/README.md) | Candidates, metatheory, proof reviews, decision records, and the arguments that failed |
| [`toolchain/`](toolchain/README.md) | Notes on the machine: a gate that misbehaves rather than fails, a tool that needs configuring once |

The two are different kinds of not-governing. `research/` is about musa and was once a live question; a decision record
there is the reason a rule in [`../rules/`](../rules/README.md) says what it says. `toolchain/` is not about musa at all
— it cannot contradict a specification, because it is describing macOS or Prettier rather than music.

Neither is an archive. Superseded designs are deleted, not moved here; what arrives here is the *argument*, written down
once the decision it supports has landed somewhere binding.
